Mission Overview

AndimaSat is a 3x1U CubeSat 1U=(10×10×10 cm) designed to demonstrate autonomous space operations and provide real‑world data for Algerian agriculture, disaster monitoring, and environmental research. The satellite will be developed over two years, with each subsystem built in parallel by specialized student teams.

Technology Demonstration

Prove low‑cost, fault‑tolerant CubeSat design using COTS components.

Agriculture Monitoring

Multispectral imaging for crop health and irrigation optimization.

Autonomous Operation

Full autonomy: detumbling, pointing, data handling, and fault recovery.

CubeSat Subsystems

Six parallel work packages

On-Board Computer (OBC)

The brain of the satellite – STM32F407 ARM Cortex‑M4, FreeRTOS, external memory, and fault‑tolerant design. Handles command execution, telemetry, and subsystem coordination.

  • 168 MHz, 1 MB Flash, 192 KB RAM
  • I²C, SPI, UART, CAN interfaces
  • Watchdog timer and software recovery
  • Open‑source firmware

Attitude Determination & Control (ADCS)

Keeps the satellite pointed correctly using magnetorquers, magnetometer, gyroscope, and control algorithms (B‑dot).

  • 3‑axis magnetometer & gyroscope (IMU)
  • Magnetorquer coils for actuation
  • Detumbling and pointing control
  • Air‑bearing table validation

Electrical Power System (EPS)

Generates, stores, and distributes power to all subsystems. Includes solar panels, battery, and power conditioning.

  • Triple junction solar cells
  • Li‑ion battery with charge controller
  • Buck/boost converters (3.3V, 5V, 12V)
  • Current/voltage monitoring

Communication System (Comms)

UHF/VHF radio link with ground station. Deployable antenna and data encoding.

  • 433 MHz / 868 MHz transceiver
  • Deployable dipole antenna (tape‑spring)
  • AX.25 / CCSDS packet protocol
  • Ground station with Yagi antenna

Structure & Thermal

Mechanical chassis, vibration isolation, thermal control, and deployment mechanisms.

  • Aluminium 6061 frame (1U standard)
  • FEA for launch loads
  • Passive thermal coating
  • Solar panel hinges

Scientific Payload

Multispectral camera for agriculture and environmental monitoring.

  • 5 MP CMOS sensor with RGB‑NIR filters
  • On‑board image compression
  • Temperature and humidity sensors
  • Data downlink upon ground request
